you're not the only one at Queen's.

We're the Queen's chapter of The Diabetes Link — a student community for anyone living with type 1, type 2, or any other kind of diabetes, plus the people who love them. Come for the snacks, stay because someone finally gets it.

What a chapter actually does

The Diabetes Link has supported young adults with diabetes since 2009. We're the version of that at Queen's — smaller, closer, and in Kingston.

Meet up

Casual hangs on and around campus where nobody has to explain what a low feels like, or apologize for a beeping pump halfway through.

Share what works

Residence fridges, exam accommodations, finding a Kingston endo, insurance after you age off a parent's plan. The practical stuff, from people a year or two ahead of you.

Dig into research

We follow what's actually happening in diabetes research and write it up in plain language, so you don't have to read a paywalled paper to find out what changed.

Make some noise

Awareness campaigns, fundraising, and campus events so the rest of Queen's has some idea what living with diabetes is actually like.

We're hiring for these roles

Every role below is open. You don't need to have diabetes to apply, and you don't need experience — most of us started with none. Tell us which one you want and why.

Website Developer

Keep this site alive: post events, update the team page, and make it look better than we left it.

Treasurer

Track the budget, handle reimbursements, and put together funding and grant applications.

First-Year Representative

Speak for first-years, help us reach residence and orientation, and tell us what a new student actually needs.

Digital Content Coordinator

Write and film the posts, reels, and recaps — captions, scripts, and the occasional interview.

Graphics Coordinator

Design posters, story templates, and merch that look like us. Canva counts.

Director of Operations and Logistics

Book the rooms, build the run sheets, and make sure events happen on time with everything they need.

Sourcing / Supply Coordinator

Find the snacks, supplies, and sponsors — and talk local businesses into helping out.

Social Media Manager

Run the Instagram: plan the calendar, post on schedule, and answer the DMs.

Apply for a role Takes about five minutes. Applications stay open until each role is filled — message us on Instagram if you'd rather ask a question first.
